The Model Registry in MLflow acts as a centralized repository designed for managing the lifecycle of machine learning
models in a professional setting. It moves beyond basic experiment tracking to provide comprehensive governance, versioning, 
and stage management.

Core Lifecycle Stages:
Development/None: The initial phase focused on active experimentation and model refinement.

Staging: A dedicated environment for validation, testing, and User Acceptance Testing (UAT) before a model is 
approved for live use.

Production: The final stage where the model is actively serving predictions.

Archived (Retirement): A critical stage for maintaining old models instead of deleting them. This is essential for:
    Auditing: Allowing teams to reconstruct past decisions, such as understanding the reasoning behind historical outcomes.
    Compliance: Meeting legal and regulatory requirements for model behavior and data handling.





Why a Registry is Essential
Complexity Management: In large organizations handling numerous projects, a registry keeps track of which model 
belongs to which project and maintains specific versioning to prevent confusion.

Version Control: It enables teams to maintain multiple versions of a model simultaneously and provides an 
easy mechanism to transition models between stages, such as promoting a top-performing candidate from development to production.
